# MPO-Core

MPO-Core is the central repository for Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO) operational resources, providing a curated collection of core files, templates, tools, guidance documents, regulatory references, and best practices used to support transportation planning, compliance, and organizational functions.

The repository serves as a consistent source of truth for MPO staff by housing standardized materials that streamline workflows, improve compliance with federal and state requirements, and promote organizational efficiency.

## Purpose

The repository is intended to:

- Act as a central hub for MPO reference materials and institutional knowledge.
- Provide standard templates for planning documents, reports, agreements, procedures, and administrative functions.
- Maintain regulatory guidance and compliance resources related to transportation planning requirements.
- Support MPO staff in the development, review, and management of planning activities.
- Promote consistency across programs, projects, and reporting processes.
- Preserve organizational knowledge and reduce duplication of effort.
- Serve as a training and onboarding resource for new staff.

## Repository Contents

Examples of resources maintained in this repository include:

- Document templates
- Planning and programming guidance
- Federal and state regulatory references
- Standard operating procedures (SOPs)
- Compliance checklists
- GIS and data management resources
- Project management tools
- Public involvement resources
- Administrative forms and workflows
- Technical reference materials

## Audience

This repository is intended for MPO staff, planners, GIS analysts, transportation professionals, consultants, partner agencies, and other stakeholders involved in the metropolitan transportation planning process.
