# Event Checklist
 
This checklist is created to help prepare for attending events for public outreach efforts by the MPO.
 
The event checklist is divided into **three categories**:
 

1. [Must bring](#must-bring)
2. [Event Specific](#event-specific)
3. [MPO Plan's Public Outreach](#mpo-plans-public-outreach)

 
Each category has its own checklist and purpose. These categories are designed to help staff and volunteers prepare for participating in events efficiently and consistently.

But before diving into the categories there, the [Pre-Event Preparation](#pre-event-preparation) and [Post-Event Follow Up](#post-event-follow-up) provide staff with tasks to prepare and guide a successful event. These sections help ensure that event planning, participation, and evaluation are conducted in a consistent and organized manner. 


## Pre-Event Preparation
- [ ] Event Registration Conformation
- [ ] Event & Booth Information
  - [ ] Confirm event Point-of-Contact
  - [ ] Confirm event location and logistics
  - [ ] Confirm setup and teardown times
- [ ] Setting Outreach Objectives
- [ ] Verify staffing assignments
- [ ] Testing Equipment:
  - [ ] Confirm Hotspot is available
  - [ ] Confirm iPad is available, updated, and working
  - [ ] Confirm Online Survey, Interactive Maps, and QR Codes are updated, printed, and active
- [ ] Event Materials:
  - [ ] Confirm MPO Brochurs updated and printed
  - [ ] Confirm MPO Plans are updated and printed
  - [ ] Confirm Travel Survey updated and printed

## Event Specific
The **Event Specific** checklist contains items and preparations that are unique to a particular event. Requirements may vary depending on the venue, audience, weather conditions, or organizer requirements.
For example, when attending Ride the Rogue - a cycling event - the MPO should focus on multimodal content and feedback from the public.

### Checklist
- [ ] Event Specific Plans / Studies
  - [ ] Grants Pass to Rogue River Multimodal Plan
  - [ ] Rogue Valley Active Transportation Plan
- [ ] Project-Specific Materials
- [ ] Display Boards or Posters
- [ ] Translation Materials

## MPO Plan's Public Outreach
The **MPO Plan's Public Outreach** checklist focuses on gathering meaningful public feedback and promoting participation in MPO planning activities. For example, collecting comments and feedbacks when updating one of the core documents. 

### Checklist
- [ ] MPO Planning Documents
  - [ ] Confirm MPO Core Plans are avialable and printed
- [ ] Public Comment Forms
- [ ] Surveys or Questionnaires
  - [ ] For example, RTP Transportation Survey
- [ ] MPO Projects Information Handouts
  - [ ] TIP Projects Mapbook
  - [ ] RTP Projects Mapbook
- [ ] Maps for Public Review
  - [ ] For example, RVMPO Bike Map.
- [ ] QR Codes for Online Resources
  - [ ] MPO websites
  - [ ] TIP Interactive Maps
  - [ ] RTP Interactive Maps

## Post-Event Follow Up
- [ ] Document collected public comments and surveys
- [ ] Organize and store event materials
- [ ] Restock supplies as needed
- [ ] Record lessons learned
- [ ] Share outreach results with MPO staff
- [ ] Archive photos and event documentation
  - [ ] Save photos in folder
  - [ ] Record comments recieved on MPO Plan or functions
