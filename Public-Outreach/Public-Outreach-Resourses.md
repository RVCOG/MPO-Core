# Public Outreach General Resourses
The following resources can help support successful public outreach efforts and improve engagement with community members. These resources provide guidance, tools, and best practices for planning outreach activities, collecting public input, communicating effectively, and ensuring meaningful public participation.

Public outreach resources may be used throughout the planning process and can assist staff in preparing for events, developing engagement strategies, gathering feedback, and documenting public involvement activities.
