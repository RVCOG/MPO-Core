# MPO Executive Orders Reference Guide

The *MPO Executive Orders Reference Guide* is a comprehensive policy reference and planning support resource that catalogs Executive Orders most relevant to Metropolitan Planning Organizations (MPOs) and Transportation Management Areas (TMAs). While Executive Orders generally do not impose direct MPO regulatory requirements in the same manner as CFRs, they frequently shape Federal Highway Administration (FHWA), Federal Transit Administration (FTA), U.S. Department of Transportation (USDOT), Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), and other federal agency policies that affect transportation planning, project development, environmental review, public engagement, equity initiatives, climate resilience, sustainability, and performance-based planning. 【1-401432】【2-3c4341】


The guide is intended to:

- **Act as a Central Hub**  
  Provide a centralized repository of Executive Orders that influence MPO planning, programming, environmental review, public involvement, performance management, resilience, and transportation investment decision-making.

- **Support MPO Staff During Daily Tasks**  
  Help MPO staff understand federal policy priorities and initiatives that may influence transportation planning requirements, project selection, public engagement activities, climate resilience strategies, and equity analyses.

- **Provide a Public Resource that Compiles MPO-Relevant Executive Orders**  
  Present Executive Orders in a structured, easy-to-understand format that enables elected officials, partner agencies, consultants, and the public to better understand the policy drivers shaping federal transportation programs.

- **Improve Policy Awareness and Understanding**  
  Help users understand how presidential priorities and federal policy initiatives influence transportation planning, environmental review, infrastructure investments, resilience planning, environmental justice, and community engagement.

- **Serve as a Training and Onboarding Resource**  
  Provide a foundational resource for MPO staff, board members, committee members, consultants, and partner agencies seeking to understand Executive Orders affecting transportation planning and policy implementation.

- **Support Planning Document Development**  
  Identify Executive Orders that may influence the development of Metropolitan Transportation Plans (MTPs), Transportation Improvement Programs (TIPs), Public Participation Plans (PPPs), environmental analyses, equity assessments, resilience plans, and performance management activities.

- **Provide Direct Access to Official Sources**  
  Include direct links to official Executive Order publications and source documents, allowing users to verify information and access authoritative policy language.

The *MPO Executive Orders Reference Guide* serves as a centralized policy reference and planning support tool for transportation practitioners, helping MPOs identify major federal policy initiatives, understand evolving planning priorities, and incorporate relevant considerations into transportation decision-making processes.

# How to Read the Executive Orders Reference Table

- **Executive Order** = Official Executive Order number.
- **Title** = Official Executive Order title.
- **Effective Date** = Date the Executive Order was signed or became effective.
- **Status** = Whether the Executive Order is Active, Historical, Revoked, Superseded, or Policy Guidance.
- **MPO Relevance** = Why MPO staff should be aware of the Executive Order.
- **Planning Documents Impacted** = MPO plans, programs, analyses, or activities potentially affected.
- **Lead Agency** = Primary implementing agency or agencies.
- **Executive Order URL** = Direct link to the official Executive Order source.
